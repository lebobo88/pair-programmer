> _"From the tail, the sword."_ The Forge crown answers the call: Daedalus shapes, Prometheus foresees, Argus watches, Hephaestus tempers, Iolaus cauterizes, the Sentinel names absences, the Seal-Bearer closes.

Run `/pp:run $ARGUMENTS`. The behavior is identical to `/pp:run` — the alias exists so the mythic vocabulary of the Hydra manifesto is addressable from the operator's keyboard. Read `.claude/commands/pp/run.md` for the canonical contract.
